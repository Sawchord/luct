# A primer on certificate transparency

luCT is built on top of an system called [certificate transparency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certificate_Transparency).
You are already using certificate transparency on a day to day basis,
porbably without knowing it.

This chapter will give a brief introduction into the components that are already in place
and secure your internet connection while you browse.
We will go through the actors in the ecosystem, how they secure your internet connection and
how you rely on them today.

The [next chapter](audit.md) will then explain, how luCT ties into this.
